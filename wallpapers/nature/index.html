Make room for your layout

A landscape subject can still be in a portrait file. Read the actual width and height on the detail page rather than assuming that every nature scene is suitable for a desktop. Crop gently so the horizon and foreground still relate.

Compare landscape desktop artwork

Color and mood

Greens and blues can coordinate with simple icon themes. Sunrise and autumn scenes offer warmer alternatives. Scene names describe artwork, not verified photographic locations.

Before you set your wallpaper

Are these photographs of real places?

The collection is landscape-inspired digital artwork. Do not use a title as evidence of a real place or a photographic capture.

Should I stretch a portrait across a monitor?

Stretching changes the shapes. Use a matching landscape file, or crop with a preview and accept that some of the image will be lost.
