Before you set your wallpaper

Are the scenes historically accurate?

No historical accuracy is asserted. The collection uses samurai-inspired figures within stylized and imagined settings.

Where should I put the clock?

Use a quiet part of the sky as a starting point, then check it on your phone; each image places its brightest areas differently.
