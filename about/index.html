FROM AN IMAGE TO AN IDEA

A practical creative workflow.

  1. Find your artwork. Search the gallery, filter by screen or theme, or follow a collection to a wallpaper page.
  2. Check the fit. Preview the artwork on a sample screen. Choose a platform format, compare the crop, and look at the original dimensions before enlarging.
  3. Make the design yours. The editor opens the original at the selected size. Keep the automatic crop, reposition it, add text, or continue with the existing template tools.
  4. Download and keep your project. Download the design and save a portable PMW project if you want to continue on another device.

Story, Reel and TikTok cover options create still images. They do not generate or edit video. Screen icons and clocks in previews are examples and stay out of downloaded artwork.

GOOD TO KNOW

Clear expectations, useful choices.

Originals and image quality

Gallery images are browsing previews. Use the original download or the editor’s original import for a design. Enlarging adds output pixels, but it cannot recover source detail. Availability can depend on the image host; if an import fails, retry or upload the original.

Personal use and permission

Read the wallpaper’s existing license before sharing a design. Business use, resale and redistribution may require permission. Material you upload remains subject to its owner’s rights.

Saving your work

Editor projects save in your browser on that device. Clearing site data or changing browsers can remove access to that local copy. Download a PMW project file for a portable copy you can reopen later.

A better fit, not just a bigger file

Choose your wallpaper resolution

Match width, height and shape to your display. Many portrait artworks here are 941 × 1672; many landscape files are 1672 × 941. Those sizes are close to 9:16 and 16:9, respectively. Each artwork page lists its actual catalog dimensions.

A phone that is taller than 9:16 may crop the sides. An ultrawide monitor may crop a 16:9 scene. Resizing to 4K makes more pixels, but it cannot recover detail missing from the original.

Before you set it

  1. Check your display size. Find its native resolution or use the wallpaper preview in your device settings.
  2. Keep the focal point. Test fill versus fit, then reposition the image.
  3. Place your labels. A clock, widgets and icon grid cover different areas.
  4. Use the original download. The gallery preview is smaller and is intended for browsing.
Dark screens, readable details

What makes an AMOLED-style wallpaper useful?

A black or nearly black field can leave space around a luminous subject. It does not guarantee a measured battery saving: OLED power depends on the actual pixels, brightness and device. Fine bright lines can still interfere with small text.

A few common questions.

Are larger exports more detailed?

No. A larger canvas changes the output dimensions. Source detail still depends on the original artwork.

Can I use the phone preview as a download?

The mockup is a screen-fit example. Use the wallpaper’s original download or the editor export to get artwork without preview overlays.

Are scene names verified places or official characters?

Artwork names describe the depicted scene. They are not claims of verified locations, astronomical observations, historical accuracy or official character affiliation.
